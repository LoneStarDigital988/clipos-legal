Data deletion and privacy requests

Email the address above with the subject “ClipOS data request.” Include your connected TikTok username or sufficient information to locate the relevant records, and describe what you want accessed, corrected or deleted. Requests are handled manually after proportionate verification of account control.

Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, source videos or sensitive identity documents. If additional verification is needed, a suitable method will be arranged separately. See the Privacy Policy for retention, backup and deletion limitations.

Disconnecting TikTok

Use Disconnect in ClipOS Publishing to clear active local credentials. Also remove ClipOS authorization in TikTok’s connected-app settings to revoke provider access. Local disconnect does not automatically delete historical records, backup copies or published posts.
