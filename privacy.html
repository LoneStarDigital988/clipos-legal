3. How information is used

TikTok data verifies the authorized account, displays its identity and maintains the connection. It is not used to infer campaign rights or publishing permission. Reconnecting or refreshing an access token does not expand the permissions the person granted.

Authorized video content is transcribed, selected and rendered locally. Owner review feedback produces bounded local selection guidance. Historical owner-supported performance and submission records inform recommendations; no model-training claim or automatic earnings verification is made. TikTok identity and tokens are not used to train AI models or sold for advertising.

4. Local storage and security

The application stores records in a local PostgreSQL database and videos in local files. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Fernet using an owner-controlled key stored separately in private application configuration. The owner password is hashed, not stored as plaintext. Application credentials are not embedded in the public legal site or public source files.

Desktop PKCE verifiers are encrypted in the connection-state record and removed when that state is consumed. The current API removes OAuth callback query strings from its Uvicorn access logs and returns callback responses with no-referrer/no-store protections. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee against device compromise, unsafe backup handling, or operator misconfiguration.

The owner can create encrypted database/configuration backups with ClipOS's backup tool and optionally include media. Backups and recovery copies remain under the owner's control; a restored copy may contain older tokens and records.

5. External requests and sharing

Local operation does not mean that no network requests occur. Authorizing TikTok sends the required authorization parameters to TikTok. The local backend exchanges the code and, when needed, refreshes tokens and sends authenticated identity requests to TikTok's official API over HTTPS. TikTok receives the relevant authorization information and network-request metadata under its own policies.

Sources or permitted campaign policy documents may be retrieved from providers the owner authorizes. Manual publication sends selected content to the platform through the operator's own action; any other separately configured integration must be disclosed before use. Cloud media storage and hosted application operation are not part of the current setup. Paid AI providers are disabled by default; the documented current video/AI workflow runs locally.

Messages sent to the public contact address are processed through Gmail, which is an external email service, rather than stored exclusively in the local application. Email correspondence may be retained while addressing the inquiry and any applicable legal or security obligations. Do not send passwords, tokens or sensitive media by email.

ClipOS has no implemented data-sale, advertising, or third-party analytics-tracking feature. The operator may share information only as specifically authorized, as needed to respond to a legitimate request or applicable legal obligation, or as otherwise disclosed before processing. Recipient platforms apply their own privacy terms.

6. Retention and deletion

No scheduled automatic purge of media, transcripts, review history, database records, or operational logs is currently implemented. These records remain until the owner removes them or carries out an applicable retention/deletion request. Retention and any requested deletion are evaluated against the actual purpose and applicable campaign or legal obligations.

OAuth state is valid for 10 minutes and a workspace session is valid for 8 hours; those validity limits do not mean expired database rows are automatically deleted. An unconsumed expired state can still contain its encrypted verifier. Tokens are retained in encrypted form while connected and refreshed according to provider expiry information; expiration alone is not a promise of immediate erasure.

Disconnect clears the current account identifier and stored credential. Display-name/username metadata, audit history, campaign records, prior publication evidence and backups may remain. Delete from Clip Review Studio archives a candidate and preserves its media and history; it is not permanent erasure. Backup copies remain until the owner manually removes them; the application does not enforce automatic backup expiration. Deleting a local record does not delete an already published social post.

Retention is owner-managed rather than governed by an automatic fixed-duration purge. Data may be retained for ongoing authorized workflows, campaign evidence, security, dispute resolution or applicable legal obligations. When processing is no longer needed, the operator handles removal manually, including review of backup copies. No fixed backup rotation or log expiration is currently enforced by the application.

7. Authorization choices, revocation and requests

You can decline authorization, decline optional profile permission, or disconnect from ClipOS. To end TikTok's authorization as well, remove ClipOS in TikTok's connected-app permissions settings. The application's local Disconnect button does not currently call TikTok's revoke endpoint. Revoking the provider grant stops authorized future access but does not automatically erase previously stored information.

Send access, correction or deletion requests to trevorderiso5@gmail.com with the subject “ClipOS data request.” Identify the connected TikTok username or other information needed to locate your records and describe the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens or identity documents unless a secure verification method is separately arranged. The operator may request proportionate proof of account control, review the request, and manually remove eligible account information, media and operational records. If information must be retained for applicable law, security or an unresolved campaign obligation, the operator will explain the reason and any limits on deletion. Backups are reviewed separately; any restored data must be subject to previously accepted deletion requests. Published posts must be removed through the relevant platform. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law; no automated deletion portal or software-enforced response deadline exists.
